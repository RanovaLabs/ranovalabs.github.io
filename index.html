How we build

Built to be trusted, then kept up.

  • 01

    One job, done well

    Each app starts from one real problem and stays focused on it. Features that don't serve that job don't ship.

  • 02

    Careful with your data

    Afterwards keeps everything on your phone. Chalkline fingerprints every photo so you can prove it hasn't been changed. Tally needs Face ID or a passcode before points are awarded.

  • 03

    Shipped and maintained

    Our apps are translated into up to twelve languages and tested before every release. Afterwards can even get factual corrections without waiting for an app update.
