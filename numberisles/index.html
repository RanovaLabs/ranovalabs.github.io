Number Isles

A gentle maths adventure for ages 4 to 7. Hatch Pip the baby dragon, feed it berries by answering questions, and explore four islands of counting, number bonds and take-away. When the islands are done, the adventure carries on in Starlight Skies.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Learn by playing

Short levels build counting to 10, making 5 and 10, part-whole and take-away, then the number line.

A friend who grows

Every right answer feeds Pip. Watch it hatch and grow, then dress it up with hats, colours and glasses.

Safe and calm

No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no in-app purchases. A calm bedtime round winds the day down.

New in version 1.1

Starlight Skies

Finished all four islands? New levels keep coming above the clouds, built around the skills your child still finds tricky, with a boss level every eighth step.

Pip's den

Spend sparkles on a rug, a comfy bed, star lights and more, then hatch dragon eggs to meet eight dragon friends who move in.

Daily quest

One short challenge a day, with a flame streak for days in a row. Miss a day and the streak simply starts again.

Crowns

Finish a level with no mistakes and quick answers to win a crown for it, plus bonus sparkles the first time.

Up to five children

Each child gets their own dragon, stars and den. A "Who's playing?" screen appears when the app opens. Add children in the grown-ups area.

A friendlier voice

Pip now uses the most natural English voice on the device. On iPhone and iPad, download an Enhanced or Premium voice for the best sound.
