Number Isles

A gentle maths adventure for ages 4 to 7. Hatch Pip the baby dragon, feed it berries by answering questions, and explore four islands of counting, number bonds and take-away.

Learn by playing

Short levels build counting to 10, making 5 and 10, part-whole and take-away, then the number line.

A friend who grows

Every right answer feeds Pip. Watch it hatch and grow, then dress it up with hats, colours and glasses.

Safe and calm

No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no in-app purchases. A calm bedtime round winds the day down.
