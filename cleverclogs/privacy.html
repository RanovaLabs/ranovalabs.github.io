Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

The short version. Clever Clogs is made for children, so it collects nothing. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no account. Everything your child enters and earns stays on the device.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Clever Clogs app for iPhone, iPad and Android ("the app") published by Ranova Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17500622, registered office 82a James Carter Road, Mildenhall, Bury St. Edmunds, England, IP28 7DE) ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. We do not receive any information from the app. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, it never sends anything to a server of ours, and it does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

What the app stores on the device

To pick the right questions and remember progress, the app saves on the device:

  • The first name your child types in, their birthday and the area where they'll take the 11+. The birthday is used only to work out their school year and the test countdown.
  • XP, levels, stars, badges, streaks and the buddy's name and look
  • Which questions were answered and how well, so difficulty can adjust
  • Settings chosen in the parent guide and on the home screen: school year, read-aloud voice, ticked guide tasks, sound and colour theme

This never leaves the device. Deleting the app, or choosing "Start again from scratch" in the parent guide, removes it. If the device is backed up by the operating system (for example iCloud or Google backup), the data may be included in that backup, which is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not ours. A first name is enough; your child doesn't need to enter a full name.

Read-aloud

Questions are read aloud by the speech engine built into the device. The app only hands it text to speak; nothing is recorded or listened to.

Purchases and links

There are no in-app purchases, no adverts and no links out of the app. The parent guide is behind a parental gate.

Children

The app is designed for children aged about 5 to 11. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to disclose, sell or delete. We built it with the UK Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code), UK GDPR and COPPA in mind.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins handling data differently, for example by adding optional family accounts, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before that version is released.
