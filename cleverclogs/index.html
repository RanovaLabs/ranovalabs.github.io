Clever Clogs

Practice games from Year 1 right up to the 11+. Children raise a buddy and cheer up grumpy monsters by answering maths, English, verbal reasoning and non-verbal reasoning questions picked for their school year.

Right for their age

Over 3,000 questions. Years 1 to 4 get gentler questions with three answers; older children work through real 11+ question types.

Read aloud

For children still learning to read, every Year 1–2 question is read out, with no timer.

Adjusts as they play

Questions get easier after mistakes and harder after a run of right answers, subject by subject.

A guide for parents

A day-by-day 11+ plan behind a parental gate, with key dates for your area.

For the whole family

Brothers and sisters share one device, each with their own buddy and progress. One 11+ unlock covers them all.

Free to start

Years 1–4 are free, with an 11+ taster. Unlock every 11+ topic and mock once, with no subscription.

Safe and private

No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Progress saves to your own iCloud, and backup files move it between devices.
